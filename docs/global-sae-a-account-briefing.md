## 3. Decision dynamics

1. **The decision unit is a family, not a committee.** The briefing is explicit:
   in fashion and construction, adoption is nearly impossible without owner-family
   backing. Optimise everything for the family saying yes in front of professional
   management.
2. **Cost conservatism is an asset.** A group that resisted expensive technology
   has a strong balance sheet and no legacy AI sprawl. Meet it with bounded scope
   and pre-agreed exit conditions.
3. **Korean proof beats global proof.** Samsung Electronics DX — ~50,000 seats,
   announced 13 July 2026, delivered via Samsung SDS — carries more weight here
   than any Western reference.
4. **The buyer is the third party at the table.** Walmart, Target, Kohl's and Gap
   are effectively present. Arguments that improve Sae-A's standing with them land.
5. **Procurement will try to shrink the first commitment.** Pre-shrink it. Offering
   1,000 seats and sixty days before they ask converts a negotiation into an
   agreement.

**Do not run this comparison in the room.** Show a competitor's genuine strength
if asked — a comparison that admits nothing is not believed by a Vice Chairman who
has evaluated vendors for a living.

## 6. Deck sequence (20 min of Google content)

No product name before the eighth beat. The first seven are about Sae-A's business.

1. **Four days ago, the number that sets your landed cost stopped existing.** Full-bleed
   map, hard line between hemispheres. Do not speak for four seconds.
2. **Almost nobody else has factories on both sides.** The flattery slide, and it's true.
3. **The constraint is Seoul, not the factories.** The pivot. Watch the owner-family CEO.
4. **Three external clocks, none of them yours.** Let KM Kim confirm the buyer-side pressure.
5. **Korea regulated AI in January; the grace period is a window.** Aimed at CTO and HR.
6. **Samsung DX, two weeks ago.** One sentence, then stop talking.
7. **Multilingual, multi-system, multi-country.** Show a competitor's real strength here.
8. **Two agents. Sixty days. One number each.** First product mention. Restraint is the message.
9. **Not seats for 70,000.** The trust slide. Deliver slowly.
10. **One cent per garment beats the whole programme.** Show the model, invite Jonghyun Lee to break it.
11. **The audit case is cash with a clean baseline.** Self-contained — the CFO will only see this one.
12. **Knowledge first, warehouse second.** Aimed at Brian Cho; converts the likely blocker.
13. **14 August: we build in the room.** The charter is the slide.
14. **What we commit, by name and date.** Close on our obligations, not theirs.
