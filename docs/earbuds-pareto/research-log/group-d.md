# Group D earbuds research (as of 2026-10-01)

## Method and limitations (read first)
- Sources: WebSearch result summaries only. WebFetch and curl to rtings.com, whathifi.com, wikipedia and others failed (no connection), so no page was read in full.
- **The session's WebSearch budget (200 calls, shared across the whole session) ran out partway through this task.** Because of that, **Sennheiser Momentum True Wireless 4, its successor Momentum True Wireless 5, and Technics EAH-AZ100 have very little data.** Most of their fields are NOT FOUND and need a follow-up pass.
- Search summaries sometimes credit a value to the wrong page. Wherever I couldn't tie a value firmly to one URL, it is flagged "(attribution uncertain)".
- RTINGS numbers: the summaries almost never showed RTINGS' numeric dB values or mic/usage scores. Several said the RTINGS microphone section reads "In Development" (RTINGS' label for a test still being updated). Numeric RTINGS fields are therefore mostly NOT FOUND.
- Evidence types: MFR = manufacturer claim, MEAS = independent lab measurement, SUBJ = reviewer opinion. "(retailer)" means the spec came from a store or spec-aggregator page, not from the manufacturer.

- Gym: IP54 handles sweat and drizzle but not swimming. No fins. With the right tips and the fit test, they held through vigorous workouts per one reviewer (https://www.well-played.com.au/nothing-ear-3-review/, attribution uncertain), but RTINGS says they can fall out [SUBJ]
- Running: needed re-seating every ~15–20 min without a fin (https://mute-zone.com/en/test/nothing-ear-3) [SUBJ]
- Office and calls: multipoint; RTINGS rates mic noise rejection a strength; Super Mic case for very noisy settings. SoundGuys disagrees (mic "muffled") [SUBJ]
- Commute and travel: strong ANC, but battery is short (5.3 h RTINGS / 5 h 12 min SoundGuys) for long flights
- Known problems: short ANC-on battery; the heavy metal case (71.4 g); treble harsh at times
