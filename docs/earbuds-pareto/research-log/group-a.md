## Gaps and conflicts

1. **Generation change:** AirPods 4 with ANC was replaced by AirPods 5 (Sept 18, 2026; $129 / $149, both with ANC). Recommend comparing **AirPods 5** going forward. AirPods 4 ANC is still sold by retailers at about $149.
2. **AirPods 5 lineup conflict:** digitalcitizen.life still lists AirPods 4 / 4 ANC at $129 / $179 and AirPods 5 at "$124.99". Apple and most outlets say $129 / $149. A non-ANC AirPods 4 Apple Store page still appeared in results.
3. **RTINGS test-bench mismatch:** the AirPods Pro 3 figures use the older "overall attenuation" format (-28.69 dB); the Powerbeats Pro 2 figures use scenario attenuation (airplane/office/street). These are not directly comparable. I could not confirm the AirPods Pro 3 -28.69 dB figure in a second query.
4. **Pixel Buds Pro 2 RTINGS battery (7 h 55 min)** is low confidence and its source is unclear.
5. **Powerbeats battery:** the manufacturer's 10 h figure is ANC-off. RTINGS measured 5.9 h continuous (probably with ANC on), which is far below the claim. The ANC-on manufacturer figure is NOT FOUND.
6. **Not found because the search budget ran out:** Powerbeats Pro 2 and Pixel Buds Pro 2 manufacturer specs (weight, IP rating, Bluetooth, codecs, multipoint, wireless charging); AirPods Pro 3 total battery, charging, Bluetooth and Find My details; all RTINGS mic scores, latency, test bench versions and review dates; What Hi-Fi, Verge and Wired star ratings; AirPods 5 RTINGS (still in testing as of Sept 18, 2026).
7. Cult of Mac flight glitch (AirPods Pro 3) and the GSMArena firmware-ANC issue: details not retrieved, and the affected model is unconfirmed for the GSMArena story.
