Patterns that matter commercially

  • Document-heavy decisioning agents (prior auth, underwriting, appeals) show 10–1000× cycle-time collapses — the fastest, best-quantified ROI anywhere.
  • Closed-loop operations agents went production in <12 months; telecom leads all industries (48% agentic adoption vs 26% cross-industry).
  • Platform-not-project buying: Citi Arc, Epic Agent Factory, ServiceNow Autonomous Workforce, Morgan Stanley super agents.
  • Voice is the production wedge in QSR and telco care — clearest labor math, highest containment.
  • The agent-to-agent economy is forming: enterprises treating other people's agents as a customer channel.

GEvidence Method

Every card carries exactly one status. The categories are never blurred; vendor marketing alone never supports DEPLOYED.

DEPLOYED

Running in production at a named company at meaningful scale — adopter-side or credible secondary source, dated.

PILOT

Named pilot, POC, or limited rollout with explicit pilot framing.

EMERGING

Repeatable pattern across ≥2 independent signals; no named production deployment yet.

SPECULATIVE

Plausible but unproven. Excluded from "ready now" rankings.

CONFIDENCE 1–5

5 = multiple named production deployments with quantified outcomes · 3 = named pilots or GA product with early customers · 1 = single promotional source.

AGENTIC BAR

The system must plan, use tools, or take multi-step autonomous action. Pure content generation or single-turn Q&A is excluded (e.g., Epic "Art" documentation is held at watchlist).
